# Shtat Lab

The DP's daily toolkit and lighting school, by [shtat.cool](https://shtat.cool).

- Open on iPhone: https://phishalili.github.io/shtat-lab/ then Safari › Share › **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Today**: next shoot countdown, sun and golden hour where you are, money in the pipeline, learning streak.
- **Shoots**: call sheets with location search, sun path, 16-day weather, crew, shot list, gear checklist, PDF call sheet.
- **Tools**: digital slate, director's viewfinder with frame lines and lens simulation, sun, lens/FOV, depth of field, shutter and flicker, ND, storage, timecode, delivery specs.
- **Clients**: projects pipeline, quotes and invoices as PDF.
- **Learn**: the Shtat Lab cinematography and lighting course (lessons, quizzes, light simulator, look library, dictionary).

Everything is stored only on the phone (localStorage). Use Settings › Export for a backup.
After changing files, bump `C` in `sw.js` so installed copies update.

Weather and place search: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com). PDFs: jsPDF (MIT). Learn photos are credited inside the app.
